Safety & responsible guidance

Fitness Guide provides general fitness and nutrition education. It does not diagnose, prescribe treatment, or replace a qualified clinician or registered dietitian. If you have a medical condition, are pregnant, have a history of an eating disorder, have unexplained symptoms, or are unsure whether exercise is appropriate, seek professional advice before following a plan.
